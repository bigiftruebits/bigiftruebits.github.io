Dashboards

Some issues come with an interactive page: the data behind the article, to explore yourself. Each one runs in your browser from the issue's own data, and the article explains what it shows.

#003

F1 ratings, 1950–2026

Every Formula 1 driver and every car rated race by race since 1950. Pick drivers, cars or both, and see how much of a driver's rating is the car.

Open the dashboard Read the article

Every dashboard is built from the issue's archived data (code and DOI on the issues page), and nothing is updated after publication.

BIG IF TRUE is made by Riccardo Gallotti (Fondazione Bruno Kessler), with analyses written and run by an AI model that every issue names. Each issue comes out in English and in Italian: ogni uscita è anche in italiano. Code: MIT. Texts, figures and derived data: each issue's licence (usually CC BY 4.0). Third-party data: their providers' licences. Found a mistake? Reply to any email, or open an issue on the issue's repository.
